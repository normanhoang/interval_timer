Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

HIIT Timer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no network connection of any kind.

Data Storage

All workouts, intervals, and session history you create are stored locally on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device — it is not uploaded to any server, and the developer has no access to it.

No Analytics or Tracking

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting services. Nothing about your usage is tracked.

Permissions

HIIT Timer does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other sensitive device data.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting the app removes all locally stored workout and history data permanently. You can also clear session history at any time from within the app's History tab.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.
